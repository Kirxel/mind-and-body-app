# Needed Brand Assets

The project intentionally avoids recreating the logo or symbol system without source files.

## Required Production Assets

- Primary logo in SVG.
- Logo mark / standalone brand sign in SVG.
- Logo with descriptor in SVG.
- Thick signage version of the logo in SVG, if distinct.
- The three geometric brand symbols in vector format.
- Any approved repeating pattern or ornament assets in vector format.

## Photography

- Approved interior photography for hero/background/space sections.
- Approved specialist portraits.
- Any detail shots that represent materials, light, and space.

## Typography

- Webfont files or exact font family names used in the brandbook.
- License confirmation for web embedding.
- Fallback typography guidance if licensed webfonts cannot be shipped.

## Color and Usage Guidance

- Full brand palette reference, including secondary blues.
- Rules for symbol usage density and spacing.
- Logo clear-space and minimum-size guidance for digital layouts.

## Delivery Notes

- Preferred filenames and export conventions.
- Whether raster exports at multiple breakpoints are also desired.
- Whether favicon/app-icon source assets are available.
